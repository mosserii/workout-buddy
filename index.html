How it works

One set, right above the prompt.

  1. You wait on Claude.

    Every second you spend waiting fills a meter, in every session at once. Quick answers never get a set.

  2. After about 5 minutes of waiting, a band appears.

    The next time Claude is busy for about 20 seconds, Clawd, the orange pixel mascot, does the exercise next to the ask: 20 squats? 1: done 2: skip

  3. Press 1 when you're done.

    Type 1 into the empty prompt. Clawd does a victory jump with sparkles and the band reads ✅ 20 squats · bravo! 🔥

  4. Or press 2 to skip.

    Nothing is logged, and the next ask is a different exercise.

When it asks

It stays out of your way.

~5 min

A waiting meter

Every second you wait on Claude counts, across all your sessions. After about 5 minutes of waiting in total, give or take a minute, a set is due.

~20 s

Only while Claude is busy

A due set waits for a turn where Claude has been working for about 20 seconds. Short prompts never trigger one.

1

One set per turn

However long the turn runs, you get one ask. Done or skipped, that's it until the next one.

Nothing pops over your work. The ask lives in the band above the prompt: no notification banners, no browser tab, no mouse. The whole answer is one key.

Exercises

Eight moves, each with its own Clawd.

Every exercise has a real animation. Plank and wall sit are held, with a countdown in the band and a drop of sweat when it gets hard.

Default ranges. Change the list and the ranges in ~/.claude/workout/config.json.

Streaks and levels

Show up daily and the sets grow.

Every 3 days of streak adds 0.1× to your reps, up to 1.5×. Here is what a 20-squat ask becomes.

Streak days 0, 3, 6, 9, 12 and 15+. Miss a day and the streak, and the multiplier, start over.

Private by design

It all stays on your machine.

Workout Buddy is a small Python script and a Claude Code mod. It never talks to the network and there is no account to make.

  • Built fromPython standard library and a Claude Code mod. Nothing else to install.
  • NetworkNone. No telemetry, no sign-in, no server.
  • Your logPlain files in ~/.claude/workout/. Delete the folder and it's gone.
  • NeedsClaude Code 2.1.287 or later, for the band above the prompt.
  • In the Desktop appThe band shows the ask and the buttons, without the pixel art.

Two commands.

This is the whole install, typed into Claude Code.

  1. Add the marketplace
    /plugin marketplace add mosserii/workout-buddy
  2. Install the plugin
    /plugin install workout-buddy@workout-buddy

Questions

Before you start.

Will it interrupt me mid-thought?

No. It only asks once Claude has been busy for about 20 seconds, when you're waiting anyway. Every second you wait, in any session, fills one shared meter. After about 5 minutes of waiting in total, give or take a minute, the next busy turn brings a set. Short prompts never trigger one, and a turn never gets more than one.

What if Claude asks me “1 or 2?”

While the band is showing, a lone 1 or 2 in an empty prompt presses its button. To answer Claude, type more than the bare digit, or wait for the band to go.

Can I pick my own exercises?

Yes. The list and each rep range live in ~/.claude/workout/config.json. The eight built-in moves each have their own animation; anything else gets a cheerful generic bounce.

How do I take a day off?

/workout pause day holds the asks until midnight, and /workout resume brings them back. A pause doesn't log anything.
